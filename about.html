Our Story

Welcome to Hot Wheels Garage, a mini online store concept made for die-cast car enthusiasts and collectors.

Our collection features sports cars, classic models and racing-inspired miniature cars. Browse the collection, search for your favourite model and add cars to your shopping cart.

This website is a student web development project. It is not affiliated with or an official store of the Hot Wheels brand.
